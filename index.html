About me

Engineering buildings for a smarter energy future

I am a PhD candidate at the Norwegian University of Science and Technology (NTNU), Department of Energy and Process Engineering, working on the design, sizing and control of smart-grid-ready building energy systems.

My research focuses on understanding how buildings can become active participants in future energy systems. This includes integrating HVAC systems, heat pumps, thermal energy storage and on-site renewable energy technologies with electricity and district heating grids.

I am particularly interested in building performance simulation, model-based control, model predictive control (MPC), energy flexibility and the practical implementation of intelligent building energy systems.

Before starting my PhD, I completed a Master's degree at NTNU, where I worked on indoor environmental quality and occupants' perceptions of the indoor environment in Norwegian schools.

My broader ambition is to contribute to sustainable, energy-efficient and comfortable buildings, with solutions that can also be adapted to the energy and building challenges in countries such as Nepal.

Research

Research focus

My work explores how building energy systems can be designed and operated to improve energy efficiency, flexibility and interaction with the wider energy system.

01 / MODELLING

Building energy modelling

Developing and integrating building and HVAC models to investigate building energy performance, system interactions and operational behaviour.

02 / CONTROL

Advanced control strategies

Investigating rule-based control and model predictive control to improve the operation of building energy systems and enable flexible energy consumption.

03 / FLEXIBILITY

Smart-grid-ready buildings

Exploring how buildings can respond to electricity prices, grid conditions and district heating requirements while maintaining indoor comfort.

04 / TECHNOLOGY

Heat pumps and thermal storage

Modelling heat pumps, thermal storage and energy conversion technologies for efficient and flexible building energy operation.

05 / RENEWABLES

Renewable energy integration

Investigating the integration of on-site renewable energy generation, storage and local energy systems to increase self-consumption and reduce emissions.

06 / IEQ

Indoor environmental quality

Understanding the relationship between building performance, indoor environmental conditions and occupants' perceived comfort and well-being.
